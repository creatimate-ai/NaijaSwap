Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about swapping your phone with verified dealers.

The top-up is simply the difference between what your current device is worth and the dealer’s selling price for your desired phone. Variables like storage capacity, cosmetic grade (Grade A or B), battery health percentage, and working Face ID / True Tone or original display determine the exact valuation.

NaijaSwap supports Apple iPhone and Samsung Galaxy swaps through verified dealer hubs.

Swaps take place safely in-person at the verified dealer’s physical store (such as Computer Village Ikeja, Banex Plaza Abuja, or Garrison Port Harcourt). This gives you the opportunity to physically inspect your new phone, verify battery health, cameras, and network unlock before paying the agreed top-up.

Stolen phones, devices locked to iCloud or Google FRP, blacklisted IMEI numbers, MDM corporate management locks, or bypass software are strictly prohibited. Every phone must be factory reset and signed out in front of the dealer during inspection.
