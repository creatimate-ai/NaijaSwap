The Three Pillars of NaijaSwap

1. Safe Deal Protocol

No meeting in unverified or unsafe locations. Every phone swap proposal is fulfilled through accredited physical inspection hubs in major tech markets like Computer Village (Lagos) and Banex Plaza (Abuja).

2. Algorithmic Fair Valuation

Eliminate endless haggling. Our valuation engine dynamically computes top-up differences based on battery cycle health, storage tier, physical grade, and real-time market index pricing.

3. Anti-Theft & IMEI Clearance

Every device submitted is cross-referenced with national IMEI blacklist databases, iCloud/FRP lock checkers, and original proof of purchase verification prior to deal clearance.
