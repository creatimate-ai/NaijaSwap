The NaijaSwap 5-Step Safe iPhone Inspection Protocol

Every iPhone trade-in on NaijaSwap is backed by our strict physical store testing criteria so you never risk buying a locked or faulty device.

1

iCloud & Apple ID Sign-Out

Both your old phone and the dealer’s phone must have iCloud / Find My completely turned off and factory reset in front of both parties. Zero locked devices allowed.

2

Face ID & True Tone Check

Dealers verify original front-facing sensor modules and genuine Apple displays so you receive a phone with 100% authentic components and working biometrics.

3

Battery Health % Verification

Settings ➔ Battery ➔ Maximum Capacity is verified on-screen. Grade A devices hold 85%+ original capacity with no battery warnings or service flags.

4

Factory Unlocked / SIM Freedom

Every phone is tested with Nigerian SIM cards (MTN, Airtel, Glo) to ensure it is completely network unlocked with zero Gevey SIM or R-SIM bypass chips.

5

Clean IMEI & 3uTools Inspection

The device IMEI is verified against stolen phone registries and 3uTools hardware diagnostic logs for total transparency and buyer peace of mind.
