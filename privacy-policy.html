01

Information We Collect

To provide a reliable, fraud-free smartphone exchange service, NaijaSwap collects limited categories of information necessary to authenticate users and verify device authenticity:

  • Account Information: Full name, verified email address, phone number (used for swap coordination and store triage verification), and profile avatar.
  • Device Technical Information: Phone make, model, storage capacity, reported cosmetic condition, battery health percentage, and photos/videos of device physical condition.
  • Unique Identifiers (Inspection Stage): International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI) and serial numbers captured strictly during physical hub triage to prevent stolen goods trafficking.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous diagnostic telemetry, app preferences, and crash reports to optimize application performance.
02

How We Use Your Information

All personal and diagnostic records collected by NaijaSwap are deployed strictly for service execution:

  • Swap Matchmaking & Algorithmic Valuation: Calculating transparent, fair cash top-up valuations between proposed devices.
  • Anti-Theft Screening: Cross-referencing device IMEI records with national carrier databases and security registries before authorizing an in-person exchange.
  • Hub Coordination: Transmitting technical specifications to certified hub technicians to expedite your 15–25 minute in-person triage.
  • Account Security: Sending transactional alerts, password reset dispatches, and login activity notices.
03

Device Privacy & Factory Reset Standard

Your personal data stored on your phone (photos, contacts, chats, financial apps) is your private property and must never enter the swap ecosystem.

⚠️ Mandatory User Obligation: Before handing over your phone at a partner hub, you MUST back up your data, sign out of your Apple ID or Google Account, disable Find My / Factory Reset Protection (FRP), and perform a complete factory wipe.

NaijaSwap, certified inspection hubs, and technicians are not liable for any residual files or personal data left on devices post-handover. Hub technicians will inspect that all cloud locks are removed before clearance.

04

Data Sharing & Disclosures

We maintain a strict zero-sale privacy standard. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or phone swap history to third-party advertisers.

Disclosures occur solely in the following controlled circumstances:

  • Certified Inspection Hubs: Relevant swap details are shared with the partner store hub where you scheduled your physical triage.
  • Law Enforcement & Crime Prevention: In cases of stolen property or fraudulent listings, device IMEI records and associated user details will be submitted to the Nigeria Police Force and law enforcement authorities.
  • Essential Infrastructure Providers: Firebase (Google LLC) for encrypted authentication and Formspree for customer support ticket dispatch.
05

Your Rights & Account Erasure

In full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and international privacy frameworks, you have sovereignty over your data:

  • Right to Rectify: You can edit your name, phone number, and avatar at any time via Account → Edit Profile.
  • Right to Erasure (Delete Account): You can permanently delete your profile, active listings, and swap records directly in Account → Danger Zone.
  • Local Cache Management: You can purge locally cached images at any time in Settings → Data & Local Storage.
06

Contact Our Data Protection Team

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your rights, or wish to report a privacy concern, our Data Protection team is ready to assist you.
