5. Role-Specific Handover Guidelines

💻 For Code & Frontend Developers

  • Import css/variables.css or css/dashboard.css in every page.
  • Always use semantic CSS variables instead of hardcoded hex values (e.g. var(--brand-primary) instead of #008751).
  • Format all prices through formatNaira(amount) to guarantee consistency across customer and dealer dashboards.
  • Ensure responsive breakpoints for mobile (<640px), tablet (640-1024px), and desktop (>1024px).

🎨 For Graphic Designers & Social Media Marketers

  • Use Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold for main headers and marketing copy.
  • Maintain minimum logo clear space (at least 20px padding around all sides of assets/logo.svg).
  • Canvas dimensions: 1080x1080 (Feed Post), 1080x1920 (Stories/Reels), 1200x630 (Web banner).
  • Always include the verified hub badges: Lagos (Computer Village) • Abuja (Banex) • Port Harcourt (Garrison).

🎬 For Video Editors & Motion Artists

  • Opening/Closing Stinger: 1.5-second animation of the dual-arrow squircle badge scaling in with spring physics, followed by an emerald bloom glow and wordmark wipe.
  • Color Grade: High contrast, dark obsidian backgrounds with neon emerald and cyan particle lighting.
  • Lower Thirds: Rounded glass badge (14px radius) with white bold text and emerald accent top-up numbers.
