Executive Summary

This security audit report presents an exhaustive review of all anti-fraud, anti-theft, and security controls implemented in the NaijaSwap application. The system deploys a multi-layered security architecture specifically tailored for Nigerian phone trade-ins: preventing stolen phone trafficking, securing funds via verified escrow, validating merchant identity (KYC), and enforcing certified physical bench triage.

1 Anti-Theft & Stolen Phone Prevention
IMEI 15-Digit Luhn Checksum Validation Anti-Theft

Validates all user-entered 15-digit IMEI numbers (*#06# check) using the mathematical Luhn algorithm to reject fabricated, mistyped, or fake IMEI strings.

  • Blocks invalid or randomly generated IMEI entries at the server boundary.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (verifyLuhnChecksum) | tests/api-server.test.js
National Stolen Device Blacklist Check Anti-Theft

Cross-references submitted IMEIs against Firestore stolen device registries (stolenDevices collection) and national carrier blacklist databases.

  • Rejects flagged devices immediately with an HTTP 403 Forbidden alert.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (/api/device/verify-imei) | firestore.rules (stolenDevices)
Active Swap Uniqueness / Duplicate Listing Lock Anti-Theft

Prevents an IMEI/serial from being listed in multiple pending or active swaps simultaneously to block parallel trading or double-swapping fraud.

  • Queries active Firestore swap requests; returns HTTP 409 Conflict if duplicate detected.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (lines 117-128) | functions/src/index.js (lines 281-288)
Mandatory iCloud / Google FRP Account Removal Anti-Theft

Mandates full sign-out of Apple ID or Google Account (disabling Find My / Factory Reset Protection) in front of the technician prior to swap clearance.

  • Strictly prohibits MDM corporate management locks, carrier financing locks, and software bypasses.
  • Implementation: index.html (Safe Deal Protocol) | faq.html | terms.html
2 Physical Bench Inspection & Certified Handover
Computer Village Physical Hub Triage Physical Security

Mandatory physical triage at certified verification benches (e.g. Computer Village Hub, Ikeja) before trade clearance.

  • Hardware checklist: Screen condition, battery health %, biometrics (TouchID/FaceID), camera functions, iCloud sign-out status, and physical IMEI matching.
  • Implementation: admin.html (Hub Desk) | api-server.js (/api/swap/inspection)
Quadruple-Lock Handover Completion Guard Escrow Protection

Strict multi-party state gate requiring four conditions before transitioning swap to completed:

  • 1. Certified technician records inspectionStatus: 'passed'.
  • 2. Customer explicitly confirms physical handover (customerConfirmed: true).
  • 3. Dealer explicitly confirms physical handover (dealerConfirmed: true).
  • 4. Cash top-up payment is verified as paid in escrow.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (lines 492-500) | functions/src/index.js (lines 509-515)
Digital Certificate of Ownership & Inspection Auditability

Issues unique certified inspection report IDs (e.g., NS-CERT-XXXXXX) stored immutably in Firestore.

  • Public verification portal (certificate.html) allows buyers/dealers to verify phone provenance.
  • Implementation: certificate.html | api-server.js (/api/certificate)
3 Merchant Verification & Identity Controls (KYC)
CAC Registration & Government ID Verification Merchant KYC

Dealers must submit CAC business registration numbers, government photo IDs, and proof of address before trading.

  • Listing Gating: Unverified dealers cannot publish listings on the platform.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (/api/dealer-verification) | dealer-verification.html
Private Storage Isolation for Sensitive KYC Documents Data Privacy

Government IDs and address proofs are restricted to authenticated private Cloudinary directories (naijaswap/{uid}/...) and protected Firebase Storage (/kyc/{uid}/).

  • Prevents public indexing or unauthorized viewing of identity documents.
  • Implementation: storage.rules | js/private-storage.js | api-server.js
4 Escrow Protection & Financial Anti-Fraud
Paystack Cash Top-Up Escrow Holding Escrow Protection

Cash top-up balances are locked in escrow (escrowStatus: 'held_in_escrow') and are not released until physical inspection passes.

  • Eliminates advance payment scams and fake bank transfer alerts.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (/api/payments/initialize & webhook)
HMAC SHA-512 Signature Webhook Validation API Security

Validates Paystack webhook events using x-paystack-signature and HMAC SHA-512 against the secret key.

  • Blocks forged payment notifications and replayed webhook payloads.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (lines 161-171)
Server-Side Amount Matching & Maximum Caps Financial Security

Compares submitted payment amounts directly against approved top-up amounts stored in Firestore; enforces maximum transaction limits (₦5,000,000 cap).

  • Prevents client-side price modification or payment tampering.
  • Implementation: api-server.js (MAX_SWAP_AMOUNT_NAIRA) | functions/src/index.js
5 Access Control & System Integrity
Granular Firestore Security Rules & Field Protection Authorization

Strict field-level controls prevent users from modifying sensitive role attributes (role, accountType, verificationStatus, admin).

  • Chat and swap records restricted exclusively to participant UIDs or verified admins.
  • State transitions constrained by immutable state machine helper functions.
  • Implementation: firestore.rules
Audit Logging & Dispute Escalation System Audit Trail

All key operations automatically create write-protected logs in auditLogs. Participants can open disputes to lock swaps for manual support review.

  • Implementation: api-server.js (/api/swap/dispute) | functions/src/index.js (logSwapActivity)
