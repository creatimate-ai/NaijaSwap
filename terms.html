01

Device Ownership & Legal Title

By listing or proposing a swap on NaijaSwap, you explicitly represent and warrant that you are the sole, lawful owner of the device, or have been expressly authorized by the legal owner to trade the device.

🚨 Zero Tolerance for Stolen Devices: Listing or attempting to swap stolen, carrier-financed, blacklisted, or iCloud/FRP locked phones is strictly prohibited. NaijaSwap actively cooperates with law enforcement and will surrender device records and user information to the Nigeria Police Force.

All swappers must be prepared to demonstrate device unlock capability and show proof of IMEI match during the physical hub triage.

02

The Safe Deal Protocol (Hub Requirement)

The hallmark of NaijaSwap is the elimination of street-corner exchanges. All swaps initiated on NaijaSwap MUST be completed in person at an authorized physical inspection hub.

  • Authorized Inspection: Certified hub technicians perform a standardized 32-point hardware and software diagnostic test on both devices.
  • No Street Meetups: Users who arrange meetings outside official partner hubs do so at their own risk and immediately forfeit all platform dispute protections, mediation rights, and fraud guarantees.
  • Neutral Ground: Hubs provide a secure, CCTV-monitored setting for testing touch responsiveness, cellular basebands, TrueTone, and battery health.
03

Algorithmic Valuation & Hub Diagnostics

The online top-up difference calculated by our algorithm is an estimate based on the swapper's declared cosmetic grade, battery wear, and model specifications.

  • Triage Discrepancies: If physical hub diagnostics reveal undisclosed defects (e.g. non-OEM screen replacement, water damage, degraded Face ID), the technician will issue an updated objective valuation.
  • Right to Withdraw: If either swapper does not agree with the technician's post-inspection valuation, either party may decline the swap with zero financial penalty or negative rating.
04

Top-Up Settlements & Handover Authorization

Top-up cash differences agreed upon between swappers must only be settled physically or via instant electronic transfer inside the partner hub once:

  • Both devices have successfully cleared the 32-point technician diagnostic checklist.
  • Both users have logged out of their Apple ID and confirmed Find My iPhone is disabled before completing a factory wipe.
  • Both swappers have signed the digital or physical Hub Exchange Clearance form.
05

Warranties & Return Standards

Warranty terms depend on the nature of the swap counterpart:

  • Certified Dealer Swaps: Phones swapped from certified partner store inventories carry a mandatory 30-day functional warranty covering motherboard, cellular baseband, and internal component defects not caused by user drops or liquid damage.
  • Peer-to-Peer Swaps: Direct consumer-to-consumer exchanges are deemed final once inspected, verified, and accepted at the hub. There are no post-handover returns for peer-to-peer exchanges once clearance is signed.
06

Prohibited Conduct & Account Sanctions

NaijaSwap reserves the right to immediately suspend or permanently terminate accounts found engaging in:

  • Listing counterfeit, replica, or cloned devices disguised as authentic smartphones.
  • Attempting to swap bypass-locked or stolen equipment.
  • Harassing, threatening, or coercing other users into unverified off-hub transactions.
  • Creating duplicate fraudulent accounts to manipulate device valuations.
